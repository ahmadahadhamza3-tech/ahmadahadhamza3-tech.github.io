Breakfast • 35 min • Medium

Aloo Paratha

Golden whole-wheat flatbread filled with gently spiced mashed potatoes, perfect with yogurt or pickle.

Ingredients

  • Whole-wheat flour — 2 cups
  • Water — about ¾ cup
  • Boiled potatoes — 2 medium
  • Green chili — 1, finely chopped
  • Fresh coriander — 2 tbsp, chopped
  • Cumin powder — ½ tsp
  • Red chili powder — ¼ tsp
  • Salt — to taste
  • Oil or ghee — for cooking

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix flour with a pinch of salt and enough water to make a soft dough; cover and rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Mash the boiled potatoes until smooth, then mix with green chili, coriander, cumin, chili powder, and salt.
  3. Divide dough and filling into 4 portions.
  4. Roll a dough portion into a small circle, place potato filling in the center, and seal the edges.
  5. Flatten gently and roll into a round paratha, using a little flour to prevent sticking.
  6. Cook on a hot tawa until small golden spots appear on both sides, brushing with oil or ghee.
  7. Serve hot with plain yogurt, raita, or pickle.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting. Cooking times can vary with equipment and ingredient size; make sure chicken is fully cooked before serving.
