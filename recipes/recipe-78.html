Fluffy Golden Belgian Waffles with Maple Butter

Crisp-on-the-outside and feather-light on the inside Belgian waffles with deep pockets to hold pools of warm maple syrup, whipped cream, and berries.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour — 2 cups
  • Cornstarch — 2 tbsp (for extra crispness)
  • Baking powder — 1 tbsp
  • Sugar — 2 tbsp
  • Eggs (whites & yolks separated) — 2
  • Whole milk (lukewarm) — 1¾ cups
  • Melted unsalted butter — ½ cup (115g)
  • Pure vanilla extract — 1 tsp
  • Pinch of salt
  • Warm maple syrup, whipped cream & mixed berries for serving

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat your Belgian waffle maker according to manufacturer instructions.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, cornstarch, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  3. In a medium bowl, whisk egg yolks, lukewarm milk, melted butter, and vanilla extract.
  4. In a clean glass bowl, whip egg whites with a hand mixer until stiff peaks form.
  5. Pour the milk-yolk mixture into the dry ingredients and stir gently just until combined (small lumps are okay).
  6. Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the batter with a spatula in two batches to keep it light and airy.
  7. Ladle batter into the preheated waffle maker and cook for 4-5 minutes until deep golden brown and crisp.
  8. Serve hot off the iron stacked high with whipped butter, pure warm maple syrup, and fresh berries.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
