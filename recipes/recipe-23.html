Fast Food • 20 min • Easy

Loaded Chili Cheese Dogs

Grilled beef frankfurters nestled in warm bakery buns smothered in hearty chili con carne and warm cheddar cheese sauce.

Ingredients

  • Jumbo beef hot dogs / franks — 4
  • Bakery hot dog buns — 4
  • Hearty beef chili — 1½ cups warmed
  • Warm cheddar cheese sauce — 1 cup
  • Red onion — ¼ cup finely diced
  • Pickled jalapeño slices — 2 tbsp
  • Yellow mustard — 2 tbsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat a grill pan or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Lightly score hot dogs with shallow diagonal cuts and grill for 5-6 minutes, turning often until nicely charred and juicy.
  3. Lightly toast the hot dog buns on the grill.
  4. Nestle each hot dog into a warm bun.
  5. Ladle warm beef chili generously over each frank.
  6. Drizzle creamy warm cheddar cheese sauce, drizzle yellow mustard, and garnish with diced red onions and jalapeños.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
