Strawberry Glazed Donuts

Soft, airy yeast donuts dipped in vibrant strawberry icing and sprinkles.

Ingredients

  • Flour — 2 cups
  • Yeast — 1 tsp
  • Sugar — 3 tbsp
  • Warm milk — ¾ cup
  • Butter — 3 tbsp
  • Powdered sugar — 1 cup
  • Strawberry puree — 2 tbsp

Cooking time

40 min

Difficulty

Medium

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare dough with flour, yeast, warm milk, sugar, and butter. Let rise for 1 hour.
  2. Roll out dough and cut into donut rings.
  3. Deep fry in warm oil until golden on both sides.
  4. Whisk powdered sugar with strawberry puree to make the glaze.
  5. Dip warm donuts into glaze, add sprinkles, and let set.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
