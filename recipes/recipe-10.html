Crispy Fried Chicken

Golden extra-crispy fried chicken wings and drumsticks seasoned to perfection.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces — 600g
  • All-purpose flour — 1½ cups
  • Cornstarch — 2 tbsp
  • Buttermilk — 1 cup
  • Garlic powder — 1 tsp
  • Paprika — 1 tsp
  • Oil for deep frying

Cooking time

35 min

Difficulty

Medium

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken in spiced buttermilk for 1 hour.
  2. Mix flour, cornstarch, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in a shallow bowl.
  3. Dredge chicken pieces thoroughly in the flour mix.
  4. Heat oil to 175°C (350°F) and fry chicken until deeply golden and cooked through (12–14 min).
  5. Drain on a wire rack and serve piping hot.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
