Pakistani • 50 min • Easy

Chicken Pulao

Fragrant Pakistani chicken pulao with tender chicken, aromatic whole spices, and fluffy basmati rice.

Ingredients

  • Chicken — 500g
  • Basmati rice — 2 cups
  • Onion — 1 large, sliced
  • Ginger-garlic paste — 1 tbsp
  • Yogurt — ½ cup
  • Cumin seeds — 1 tsp
  • Black peppercorns — 6
  • Bay leaf — 1
  • Oil — 3 tbsp
  • Salt — to taste
  • Water or stock — about 3 cups

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Rinse rice until the water runs mostly clear, then soak for 20 minutes and drain.
  2. Heat oil in a heavy pot and fry the onion until golden.
  3. Add cumin, peppercorns, bay leaf, ginger-garlic paste, and chicken; cook for 5–7 minutes.
  4. Stir in yogurt and salt, then cook until the chicken is lightly browned.
  5. Add drained rice and gently stir to coat the grains with the spices.
  6. Pour in hot water or stock, bring to a boil, then cover and cook on low for 15–18 minutes until rice is tender.
  7. Rest covered for 10 minutes, fluff gently, and serve with raita.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting. Cooking times can vary with equipment and ingredient size; make sure chicken is fully cooked before serving.
