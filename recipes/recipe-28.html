Shahi Haleem (Slow-Cooked Stew)

Royal slow-simmered hearty stew of shredded beef, broken wheat, barley, and mixed lentils cooked to silky perfection with authentic spices.

Ingredients

  • Boneless beef shank or mutton — 600g
  • Broken wheat (dalia) & Pearl barley — 1 cup soaked
  • Mixed lentils (chana, masoor, moong, mash) — 1 cup soaked
  • Shahi Haleem spice blend — 3 tbsp
  • Ginger garlic paste — 2 tbsp
  • Fried golden onions (Birista) — 1 cup
  • Desi Ghee — ½ cup
  • Julienned ginger, chopped mint, green chilies, lemon wedges & chaat masala for topping

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Soak wheat, barley, and mixed lentils overnight. Pressure-cook with water and turmeric until tender, then coarsely blend or mash.
  2. In a separate pot, heat ghee and sear meat with ginger garlic, whole spices, and Haleem masala blend until oil separates.
  3. Add water and cook meat until meltingly tender. Shred meat finely using two forks or wooden masher.
  4. Combine shredded meat and cooked lentil-wheat mixture in a large heavy pot.
  5. Cook on low heat, pounding continuously with a wooden masher (ghotna) for 30 minutes until silky, thick, and stretchable.
  6. Prepare aromatic tarka: Sauté sliced onions in hot desi ghee until golden brown and pour sizzling over the haleem.
  7. Serve hot topped with fresh ginger strips, chopped mint, lemon wedges, green chilies, and a sprinkle of chaat masala.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before cooking and adjust seasoning gradually. Cooking time can vary with equipment and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
