Chicken Manchurian with Egg Fried Rice

Crispy chicken bites tossed in sweet, spicy, and tangy Indo-Chinese garlic sauce.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken cubes — 400g
  • Cornstarch — 3 tbsp + Egg — 1
  • Garlic — 2 tbsp finely chopped
  • Ginger — 1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce & Chili garlic sauce — 2 tbsp each
  • Tomato ketchup — 3 tbsp
  • Capsicum & spring onion — ½ cup chopped
  • Chicken broth — 1 cup

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Coat chicken with egg, cornstarch, salt, and pepper. Deep fry or pan fry until golden and crispy.
  2. In a wok, heat 2 tbsp oil, stir-fry chopped garlic and ginger for 30 seconds on high heat.
  3. Add capsicum and spring onions, sauté for 1 minute.
  4. Add soy sauce, ketchup, chili garlic sauce, vinegar, and chicken broth. Bring to a boil.
  5. Stir in cornstarch slurry (1 tbsp cornstarch in 2 tbsp water) until sauce thickens to a glossy glaze.
  6. Toss in the crispy chicken bites and coat thoroughly.
  7. Serve hot with wok-tossed egg fried rice.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
