Creamy Garlic Pasta

Creamy garlic pasta with cheese for a quick comfort dinner.

Ingredients

  • Pasta — 250g
  • Cream — 1 cup
  • Cheese — ½ cup
  • Garlic — 2 cloves
  • Butter — 1 tbsp
  • Black pepper

Cooking time

30 min

Difficulty

Easy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil pasta until tender.
  2. Melt butter and sauté garlic.
  3. Add cream and black pepper.
  4. Stir in cheese.
  5. Add pasta and toss until coated.
  6. Serve immediately.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
