Greek Yogurt Berry Honey Crunch Bowl

High-protein thick Greek yogurt layered with organic berries, chia seeds, roasted almonds, and raw honey.

Ingredients

  • Plain Greek yogurt (0% or 2%) — 1½ cups
  • Fresh strawberries & blueberries — 1 cup
  • Toasted granola — ¼ cup
  • Chia seeds & flax seeds — 1 tbsp each
  • Sliced roasted almonds — 2 tbsp
  • Raw organic honey — 1½ tbsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Spoon chilled thick Greek yogurt into a wide breakfast bowl.
  2. Arrange fresh sliced strawberries and blueberries neatly across one side.
  3. Add toasted granola, sliced almonds, and a sprinkle of chia seeds.
  4. Drizzle golden raw honey over the entire bowl.
  5. Serve immediately for an energetic protein-packed meal.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
