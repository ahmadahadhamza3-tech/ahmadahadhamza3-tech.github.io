Pakistani • 60 min • Medium

Lahori Chargha (Whole Fried Chicken)

Steamed then deep-fried whole chicken marinated in tangy Lahori spices and lemon juice.

Ingredients

  • Whole skinless chicken with deep cuts — 1.2kg
  • Thick yogurt — ½ cup
  • Lemon juice — 3 tbsp
  • Lahori Chargha masala — 3 tbsp
  • Egg — 1 beaten
  • Ginger garlic paste — 2 tbsp
  • Chaat masala for dusting
  • Oil for deep frying

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Whisk yogurt, lemon juice, ginger garlic, Chargha masala, egg, and salt into a thick marinade.
  2. Rub marinade deep into chicken cuts and let rest for at least 3 hours or overnight.
  3. Place marinated chicken in a steamer basket and steam over boiling water for 25 minutes until cooked through.
  4. Remove and let cool completely on a wire rack so excess moisture evaporates.
  5. Heat oil in a wok to 180°C and deep fry whole chicken for 6-8 minutes until deeply golden and crispy on all sides.
  6. Dust generously with chaat masala and serve with tamarind sauce and parathas.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
