BBQ Chicken Quesadilla

Crispy toasted flour tortilla filled with smoky BBQ pulled chicken, sweet corn, and melted Monterey Jack.

Ingredients

  • Flour tortillas — 2 large (10-inch)
  • Cooked shredded chicken — 1½ cups
  • Sweet smoky BBQ sauce — 3 tbsp
  • Cheddar or Monterey Jack cheese — 1½ cups shredded
  • Sweet corn kernels — ¼ cup
  • Red onion — 2 tbsp finely diced
  • Fresh cilantro — chopped
  • Butter — 1 tbsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Toss shredded chicken with BBQ sauce, diced red onion, and sweet corn in a bowl.
  2. Heat a large flat skillet over medium heat and melt a dab of butter.
  3. Place one flour tortilla in the pan. Sprinkle half the cheese across one side.
  4. Layer the BBQ chicken filling evenly over the cheese, add remaining cheese, and fold tortilla over into a half-moon.
  5. Cook for 3-4 minutes per side pressing gently with a spatula until golden brown and cheese is completely melted.
  6. Cut into 3 wedges and serve with sour cream or salsa.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
