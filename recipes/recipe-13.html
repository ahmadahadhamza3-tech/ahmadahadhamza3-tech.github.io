Pakistani • 50 min • Medium

Chicken Nihari (Authentic Lahore Style)

Tender chicken drumsticks and thighs slow-simmered in a rich, velvety aromatic stew infused with whole spices and roasted flour gravy.

Ingredients

  • Chicken drumsticks & thighs — 800g
  • Special Nihari masala blend — 3 tbsp
  • Wheat flour (atta) — 3 tbsp roasted & mixed with water
  • Desi Ghee or mustard oil — ½ cup
  • Ginger garlic paste — 2 tbsp
  • Fried onions (Birista) — ½ cup
  • Ginger juliennes, sliced green chilies, cilantro & fresh lemon wedges for garnish

Cooking time

50 min

Difficulty

Medium

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot and sear chicken pieces over medium-high heat until lightly golden.
  2. Add ginger garlic paste and Nihari masala blend; sauté for 3-4 minutes until fragrant oil releases.
  3. Pour in 4 cups of hot water, cover tightly, and simmer on low flame for 35 minutes until chicken is meltingly tender.
  4. Gradually whisk in the roasted wheat flour slurry while stirring continuously to create a smooth, glossy, thick gravy.
  5. Simmer on low heat (Dum) for 10-12 minutes until aromatic red oil (tari) floats to the surface.
  6. Serve hot garnished with julienned fresh ginger, chopped green chilies, coriander, and freshly squeezed lemon juice alongside warm tandoori naan.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
