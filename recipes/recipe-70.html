Dessert • 25 min • Medium

Warm Molten Chocolate Lava Cake

Individual dark chocolate cakes with delicate firm edges that break open into an irresistible warm, flowing molten chocolate center.

Ingredients

  • Semi-sweet or bittersweet dark chocolate (60-70%) — 120g chopped
  • Unsalted butter — ½ cup (115g)
  • Powdered sugar — ½ cup
  • Eggs — 2 whole + 2 egg yolks
  • All-purpose flour — 3 tbsp
  • Pure vanilla extract — 1 tsp
  • Pinch of fine sea salt
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder for dusting ramekins
  • Vanilla bean ice cream & fresh raspberries

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 220°C (425°F). Butter four 6-ounce ramekins generously and dust with cocoa powder, tapping out excess.
  2. In a microwave-safe bowl or double boiler, melt chopped dark chocolate and butter together until smooth and glossy.
  3. Whisk in powdered sugar and salt until well incorporated.
  4. Add whole eggs, egg yolks, and vanilla extract; whisk gently until combined and velvety.
  5. Fold in all-purpose flour with a spatula just until no dry pockets remain (do not overmix).
  6. Divide batter equally among prepared ramekins.
  7. Bake for 12-14 minutes until the edges are firm and cake-like, but the center is still soft and slightly jiggly.
  8. Run a small knife around the edges, place an inverted dessert plate on top, and flip quickly. Let sit for 10 seconds, then lift ramekin. Dust with powdered sugar and serve with vanilla ice cream.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
