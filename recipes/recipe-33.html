Chicken • 35 min • Easy

Chicken Malai Boti Skewers

Supremely tender and creamy chicken breast chunks marinated in thick cream, green chilies, and white pepper.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken breast — 500g cubes
  • Heavy cream — 3 tbsp
  • Thick Greek yogurt — 3 tbsp
  • Green chili paste — 1 tbsp
  • Garlic ginger paste — 1 tbsp
  • White pepper & cumin powder — 1 tsp each
  • Melted butter for basting

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix heavy cream, yogurt, green chili paste, garlic ginger, white pepper, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Coat chicken cubes thoroughly and marinate for at least 2 hours (or overnight).
  3. Thread chicken onto soaked wooden skewers.
  4. Grill on a hot grill pan or barbecue for 8-10 minutes, turning and basting with melted butter.
  5. Char lightly for a smoky restaurant finish.
  6. Serve with mint raita and soft naan.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
