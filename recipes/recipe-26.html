Degi Mutton Yakhni Pulao

Fragrant basmati rice slow-cooked in rich whole-spice mutton bone broth with caramelized onions.

Ingredients

  • Mutton meat on the bone — 750g
  • Aged long-grain basmati rice — 500g
  • Onions — 2 large sliced
  • Yogurt — ½ cup
  • Fennel seeds & Coriander seeds — 1½ tbsp each (in spice pouch)
  • Whole spices (cinnamon, cloves, black cardamom, star anise)
  • Ghee — ½ cup
  • Ginger garlic paste — 2 tbsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil mutton with 6 cups water, spice pouch (fennel, coriander, garlic, onion, whole spices) for 45 minutes to prepare rich Yakhni broth. Strain and keep broth.
  2. In a separate heavy pot, heat ghee and fry sliced onions until deep golden brown. Save half for garnish.
  3. Add ginger garlic paste, cooked mutton pieces, and yogurt. Sauté for 6-8 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Pour the strained mutton broth into the pot and bring to a rolling boil.
  5. Add soaked basmati rice and cook on high heat until 80% of the liquid is absorbed.
  6. Cover with a tight lid on lowest heat (Dum) for 15 minutes.
  7. Fluff gently and serve with cucumber mint raita.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before cooking and adjust seasoning gradually. Cooking time can vary with equipment and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
