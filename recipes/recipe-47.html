Breakfast • 40 min • Medium

Lahori Halwa Puri & Chana Platter

The quintessential Lahore breakfast feast: crispy puffed golden puris served with spicy chickpea curry, sweet cardamom semolina halwa, and tangy pickle.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour (maida) — 2 cups
  • Semolina (sooji) — 1 cup
  • Sugar — 1 cup
  • Desi Ghee — ½ cup
  • Boiled chickpeas — 2 cups with cooking broth
  • Kalonji (nigella seeds) & cumin — 1 tsp each
  • Green cardamom pods — 4
  • Yellow food color or saffron — a pinch
  • Pickled mango & green chilies — for serving
  • Oil — for deep frying puris

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Knead smooth, soft puri dough using flour, salt, oil, and lukewarm water. Rest for 20 minutes.
  2. Prepare Shahi Halwa: Roast semolina in desi ghee over medium heat until aromatic. Slowly add boiling cardamom sugar syrup and stir continuously until thick, glossy, and ghee separates.
  3. Prepare Lahori Chana: Sauté kalonji, cumin seeds, ginger garlic paste, tomatoes, and red chili in ghee. Add boiled chickpeas with their broth, crush a few chickpeas to thicken gravy, and simmer for 15 minutes with fenugreek and black pepper.
  4. Divide puri dough into small balls, grease lightly, roll very thin, and fry in smoking hot oil for 6-8 seconds until magically puffed and golden.
  5. Serve hot puffed puris immediately with the warm sweet halwa, spicy tarkeydar chana, and sour Lahori achar.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
