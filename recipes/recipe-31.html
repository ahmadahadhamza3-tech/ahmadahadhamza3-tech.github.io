Chicken • 35 min • Easy

Creamy Chicken Handi

Boneless chicken cubes cooked in a traditional claypot gravy with yogurt, cream, and roasted coriander.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken breast / thighs — 500g cubes
  • Yogurt — ½ cup whisked
  • Fresh cooking cream — 4 tbsp
  • Onion paste — 1 cup
  • Ginger garlic paste — 1½ tbsp
  • Roasted crushed coriander & cumin — 1 tbsp each
  • White pepper powder — ½ tsp
  • Butter / Ghee — 3 tbsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat ghee in a handi or heavy pan and fry onion paste until golden.
  2. Add ginger garlic paste and chicken cubes; fry until chicken changes color.
  3. Lower flame, stir in whisked yogurt, white pepper, crushed roasted cumin, and coriander.
  4. Cover and cook on low heat for 12 minutes until chicken is tender.
  5. Pour in cooking cream and simmer for 3 minutes until oil separates.
  6. Garnish with ginger juliennes, green chilies, and serve with hot butter kulchas.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
