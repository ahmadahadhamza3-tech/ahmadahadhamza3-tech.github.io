Chicken • 40 min • Medium

Crispy Chicken Parmesan (Parmigiana)

Crispy herb-panko crusted chicken cutlets smothered in rich marinara, fresh basil, and bubbling melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast cutlets — 2 large (pounded to ½ inch)
  • Italian seasoned panko breadcrumbs — 1 cup
  • Grated Parmesan cheese — ½ cup
  • Eggs — 2 beaten
  • All-purpose flour — ½ cup
  • Marinara sauce — 1½ cups
  • Fresh mozzarella cheese — 150g sliced or shredded
  • Fresh basil leaves — ¼ cup
  • Olive oil for shallow frying

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Set up breading station: Flour in bowl 1, beaten eggs in bowl 2, and panko mixed with grated parmesan and Italian herbs in bowl 3.
  3. Dredge chicken cutlets in flour, dip in beaten eggs, and press firmly into panko mixture for full coverage.
  4. Heat ¼ inch of olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Fry cutlets for 3-4 minutes per side until deeply golden and crispy. Drain on paper towels.
  5. Spread ½ cup of marinara sauce in the bottom of a baking dish. Place fried chicken cutlets in the dish.
  6. Top each cutlet with remaining marinara sauce, fresh mozzarella slices, and a sprinkle of parmesan.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbling with golden brown spots. Garnish with fresh torn basil.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
