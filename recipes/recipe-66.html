Greek Lemon Garlic Roasted Chicken & Potatoes

Golden crisp bone-in chicken thighs and Yukon gold potato wedges roasted in a fragrant olive oil, freshly squeezed lemon juice, garlic, and wild mountain oregano bath.

Ingredients

  • Bone-in chicken thighs — 6 pieces
  • Yukon gold potatoes — 4 large cut into wedges
  • Extra virgin olive oil — ⅓ cup
  • Fresh lemon juice — ⅓ cup (about 2 large lemons)
  • Garlic cloves — 6 minced
  • Dried Greek oregano — 1½ tbsp
  • Chicken stock — ½ cup
  • Sea salt & cracked black pepper — 1 tsp each
  • Fresh parsley & lemon slices for serving

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. In a glass measuring jug, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, chicken stock, minced garlic, oregano, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Arrange potato wedges and chicken thighs in a single layer in a large baking dish.
  4. Pour the lemon-garlic marinade evenly over the chicken and potatoes, tossing potatoes lightly to coat.
  5. Roast uncovered for 45-50 minutes, basting chicken and potatoes once halfway through, until chicken skin is blistered and deeply crisp, and potatoes are tender and golden.
  6. Switch broiler on high for 2-3 minutes if extra crispy skin is desired.
  7. Garnish with fresh lemon slices and chopped parsley. Serve with warm pita bread and tzatziki.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
