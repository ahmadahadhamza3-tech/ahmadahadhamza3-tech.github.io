Pakistani • 35 min • Medium

Royal Beef Seekh Kabab

Juicy spiced minced beef skewers charcoal-grilled with aromatic herbs and garam masala.

Ingredients

  • Minced beef (20% fat) — 600g
  • Finely chopped onion — 1 (squeezed dry)
  • Ginger garlic paste — 1½ tbsp
  • Green chilies — 3 finely minced
  • Gram flour (roasted besan) — 2 tbsp
  • Coriander & Cumin powder — 1 tbsp each
  • Garam masala — 1 tsp
  • Fresh mint & cilantro — ½ cup chopped

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Combine minced beef, squeezed onion, ginger garlic, green chilies, herbs, and roasted gram flour in a bowl.
  2. Knead mixture firmly with your hands for 8-10 minutes until sticky and uniform.
  3. Chill in refrigerator for 30 minutes.
  4. Mold seasoned beef around flat metal skewers into cylindrical kababs.
  5. Grill over hot coals or cook in a hot cast iron grill pan with butter for 8-10 minutes, turning regularly.
  6. Smoke with a live charcoal and a drop of ghee for 2 minutes for authentic BBQ aroma.
  7. Serve hot with mint chutney, ring onions, and fresh tandoori naan.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
