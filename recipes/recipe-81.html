Mango Lassi

A cool, creamy mango and yogurt drink with a hint of cardamom for warm afternoons.

Ingredients

  • Ripe mango — 1 large, peeled and chopped
  • Plain yogurt — 1 cup
  • Cold milk — ½ cup, more as needed
  • Sugar or honey — 1–2 tsp, optional
  • Ground cardamom — a small pinch
  • Ice cubes — optional

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Add mango, yogurt, milk, and optional sugar to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy, adding a little more milk if you prefer a thinner drink.
  3. Taste and adjust sweetness as needed.
  4. Add a pinch of cardamom and pulse briefly to combine.
  5. Pour into chilled glasses and serve immediately.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting. Cooking times can vary with equipment and ingredient size; make sure chicken is fully cooked before serving.
