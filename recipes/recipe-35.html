Dessert • 45 min • Medium

Zafrani Shahi Kheer (Rice Pudding)

Rich, slow-simmered basmati rice dessert infused with saffron strands, green cardamom, and toasted pistachios.

Ingredients

  • Whole full-fat milk — 1.5 liters
  • Basmati rice — ¼ cup (soaked & crushed)
  • Sugar — ½ cup (to taste)
  • Saffron strands — 15 steeped in warm milk
  • Cardamom powder — ½ tsp
  • Slivered almonds & pistachios — ¼ cup
  • Rose water or Kewra water — 1 tsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Soak basmati rice for 30 minutes, then coarsely crush with your fingers.
  2. Bring whole milk to a boil in a heavy-bottomed pot.
  3. Add crushed rice and simmer on low heat for 30-35 minutes, stirring frequently so it doesn't stick.
  4. When rice is creamy and milk has reduced by a third, stir in sugar, cardamom powder, and saffron milk.
  5. Cook for another 8-10 minutes until thick and luscious.
  6. Stir in rose water and half the toasted nuts.
  7. Pour into clay serving bowls, chill in refrigerator for 2 hours, and top with remaining slivered pistachios and edible silver foil.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
