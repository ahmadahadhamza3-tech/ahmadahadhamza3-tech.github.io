Pakistani • 30 min • Medium

Peshawari Chapli Kabab

Traditional Khyber Pakhtunkhwa flat patties packed with coarsely crushed pomegranate seeds, coriander, tomatoes, and fried in pure desi ghee.

Ingredients

  • Minced beef or lamb (with 20% fat) — 500g
  • Ripe tomatoes — 2 (1 finely chopped, 1 sliced into thin rounds)
  • Medium onion — 1 finely minced & moisture squeezed out
  • Green chilies — 3-4 finely chopped
  • Fresh coriander & mint — ½ cup chopped
  • Anardana (dried pomegranate seeds) — 1½ tbsp crushed
  • Coarsely crushed coriander seeds — 1½ tbsp
  • Roasted cumin powder & garam masala — 1 tsp each
  • Makki ka atta (fine yellow cornmeal) — 3 tbsp
  • Egg — 1 beaten
  • Desi ghee or tallow for shallow frying

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine minced meat, squeezed onions, chopped tomatoes, green chilies, coriander, and mint.
  2. Add crushed anardana, crushed coriander seeds, cumin, garam masala, salt, cornmeal, and beaten egg.
  3. Knead vigorously with your hands for 6-8 minutes until the meat mixture turns sticky and well-bound.
  4. Divide into large balls. Flatten each ball thinly on your palm into a wide disc. Press a round tomato slice firmly into the center.
  5. Heat ghee in a wide flat iron tawa or skillet over medium heat.
  6. Slide the chapli kababs into the hot ghee. Fry for 4-5 minutes per side, gently spooning hot ghee over the tomato side until deeply browned and crispy on the edges.
  7. Serve immediately with naan, onion rings, and sour tamarind chutney.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
