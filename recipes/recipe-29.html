Pakistani • 30 min • Easy

Dhaba Style Dal Tadka

Creamy yellow lentils simmered with aromatics and finished with a sizzling desi ghee tarka of cumin, sliced garlic, and whole red chilies.

Ingredients

  • Toor dal & Moong dal — 1 cup rinsed
  • Ripe tomatoes — 2 finely chopped
  • Onion — 1 finely diced
  • Desi Ghee — 3 tbsp
  • Garlic cloves — 6 thinly sliced
  • Cumin seeds — 1 tsp
  • Dried whole red chilies — 3
  • Kashmiri red chili powder & turmeric — 1 tsp each
  • Hing (asafoetida) — a pinch
  • Kasoori methi — 1 tsp
  • Fresh coriander — chopped

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Pressure-cook toor and moong dal with turmeric, salt, and 3 cups of water for 12 minutes until soft and creamy. Whisk gently.
  2. In a heavy skillet, melt 1 tbsp ghee, sauté diced onions until translucent, add chopped tomatoes, and cook until mashed into a spiced masala.
  3. Pour cooked lentils into the onion-tomato masala and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Crush kasoori methi between palms and stir into simmering dal.
  5. Prepare smoky double tadka: In a small pan, heat 2 tbsp desi ghee. Add sliced garlic until golden, add cumin seeds, dried red chilies, hing, and Kashmiri chili powder.
  6. Immediately pour the sizzling fragrant tadka over the dal and cover with lid for 2 minutes to trap the smoke and aroma.
  7. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve piping hot with steamed basmati rice or tandoori roti.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before cooking and adjust seasoning gradually. Cooking time can vary with equipment and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
