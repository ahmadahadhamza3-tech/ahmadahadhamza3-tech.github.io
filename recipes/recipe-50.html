Creamy Tuscan Garlic Chicken

Pan-seared golden chicken breasts in a rich garlic, sun-dried tomato, baby spinach, and parmesan cream sauce.

Ingredients

  • Boneless skinless chicken breasts — 2 (cut horizontally)
  • Heavy whipping cream — 1 cup
  • Sun-dried tomatoes in oil — ½ cup drained & sliced
  • Fresh baby spinach — 2 cups packed
  • Garlic cloves — 4 minced
  • Chicken broth — ½ cup
  • Grated Parmesan cheese — ½ cup
  • Olive oil & Butter — 1 tbsp each
  • Italian seasoning — 1 tsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Season chicken breasts with salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  2. Heat olive oil and butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken for 5-6 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
  3. In the same skillet, sauté minced garlic for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream; bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Stir in grated Parmesan and sliced sun-dried tomatoes until the sauce is creamy and smooth.
  6. Add fresh baby spinach and simmer for 2 minutes until wilted.
  7. Return cooked chicken breasts and rested juices back into the skillet, spooning the velvety sauce over top. Serve hot with pasta, rice, or crusty bread.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
