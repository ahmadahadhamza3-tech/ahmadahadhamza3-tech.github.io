Shakshuka (Eggs in Tomato Sauce)

Poached eggs simmering in a rich spiced sauce of tomatoes, bell peppers, and cumin.

Ingredients

  • Eggs — 4
  • Crushed tomatoes — 400g
  • Bell pepper — 1 chopped
  • Onion — 1 diced
  • Garlic — 2 cloves
  • Cumin & Paprika — 1 tsp each
  • Fresh parsley & feta

Cooking time

25 min

Difficulty

Easy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sauté onions, garlic, and bell peppers in olive oil until soft.
  2. Add crushed tomatoes, cumin, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Use a spoon to create small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each well.
  4. Cover and cook on low heat for 5-8 minutes until egg whites are set but yolks remain soft.
  5. Garnish with parsley and feta, serve with warm crusty bread.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
