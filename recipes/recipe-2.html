Chicken Karahi

A rich, spicy tomato chicken curry made in classic karahi style.

Ingredients

  • Chicken — 500g
  • Tomatoes — 4
  • Green chilies — 4
  • Ginger — 1 tbsp
  • Garlic — 1 tbsp
  • Oil — 4 tbsp
  • Red chili powder — 1 tsp

Cooking time

45 min

Difficulty

Easy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a karahi.
  2. Add chicken and fry lightly.
  3. Add ginger and garlic.
  4. Add tomatoes and spices.
  5. Cook until the tomatoes break down and chicken is tender.
  6. Finish with green chilies and ginger.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
