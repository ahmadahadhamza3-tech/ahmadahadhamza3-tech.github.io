Fluffy Garden Herb & Cheese Omelette

French-style soft folded eggs filled with creamy goat cheese or cheddar, chives, and cherry tomatoes.

Ingredients

  • Fresh eggs — 3
  • Butter — 1½ tbsp
  • Milk — 1 tbsp
  • Grated cheddar or goat cheese — ¼ cup
  • Fresh chives & parsley — 2 tbsp minced
  • Cherry tomatoes — 4 halved
  • Salt & freshly cracked black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Whisk eggs with milk, salt, and pepper with a fork until completely blended and smooth.
  2. Melt butter in an 8-inch non-stick skillet over medium-low heat.
  3. Pour in eggs and swirl gently, pulling cooked edges toward center so runny egg fills the gaps.
  4. When top is softly set but still creamy, sprinkle cheese, herbs, and tomatoes across one half.
  5. Fold omelette over in half and slide onto a warm plate.
  6. Brush top with a touch of melted butter and sprinkle extra fresh herbs.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
