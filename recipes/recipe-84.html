Chocolate Chip Cookies

Soft-centered cookies with golden edges and plenty of melted chocolate chips.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour — 1½ cups
  • Unsalted butter, softened — ½ cup
  • Brown sugar — ½ cup
  • White sugar — ¼ cup
  • Egg — 1
  • Vanilla extract — 1 tsp
  • Baking soda — ½ tsp
  • Salt — ¼ tsp
  • Chocolate chips — ¾ cup

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a baking tray with parchment.
  2. Beat softened butter with brown and white sugar until creamy.
  3. Mix in the egg and vanilla extract.
  4. In a separate bowl, combine flour, baking soda, and salt; stir into the wet mixture just until combined.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips and scoop tablespoon-sized portions onto the tray, leaving space between them.
  6. Bake for 9–12 minutes until the edges are golden and the centers still look slightly soft.
  7. Cool on the tray for 5 minutes, then move to a rack to finish cooling.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting. Cooking times can vary with equipment and ingredient size; make sure meat and poultry are fully cooked before serving.
