Crispy Chicken Shawarma Wrap

Spiced Middle Eastern shredded chicken wrapped in pita with garlic toum sauce, pickles, and crispy fries.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken thighs — 400g thinly sliced
  • Shawarma spice blend (cumin, paprika, cinnamon, garlic) — 1½ tbsp
  • Olive oil & lemon juice — 2 tbsp each
  • Pita or flatbreads — 3
  • Garlic Toum sauce or Mayo — 4 tbsp
  • Pickled cucumbers — sliced
  • French fries — 1 cup

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken slices in shawarma spices, olive oil, lemon juice, and garlic for 20 minutes.
  2. Pan-sear on high heat in a cast-iron skillet for 6-8 minutes until nicely charred and juicy.
  3. Warm pita bread on the griddle.
  4. Spread a generous layer of garlic toum sauce across the warm pita.
  5. Fill with sliced shawarma chicken, crunchy pickles, and a handful of hot french fries.
  6. Roll tightly, toast on the pan for 1 minute for a crispy outer crust, and slice diagonally.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
