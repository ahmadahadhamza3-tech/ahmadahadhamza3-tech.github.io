Cheesy Pepperoni Pizza

Crispy crust pizza baked with rich herb tomato marinara, melted mozzarella, and spicy beef pepperoni slices.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough — 1 ball (300g)
  • Pizza tomato sauce — ½ cup
  • Shredded mozzarella — 1½ cups
  • Pepperoni slices — 20 pieces
  • Olive oil — 1 tbsp
  • Dried oregano & basil — 1 tsp
  • Grated parmesan — 2 tbsp

Cooking time

30 min

Difficulty

Medium

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven with pizza stone or baking tray to highest setting (240°C / 475°F).
  2. Stretch pizza dough on a lightly floured surface into a 12-inch round circle.
  3. Spread seasoned tomato pizza sauce evenly, leaving a ½ inch crust border.
  4. Sprinkle generous layer of mozzarella cheese and arrange pepperoni slices on top.
  5. Brush crust edges lightly with olive oil and sprinkle dried oregano.
  6. Bake for 10-12 minutes until crust is blistered golden and cheese is bubbling with toasted spots.
  7. Slice into wedges and enjoy hot.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
