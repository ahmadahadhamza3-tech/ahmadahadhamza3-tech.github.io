Chicken • 35 min • Medium

Korean Sticky Gochujang Fried Chicken (Yangnyeom)

Double-fried extra crunchy chicken bites coated in a spicy-sweet fermented Korean gochujang, honey, garlic, and toasted sesame glaze.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken thighs or wings — 650g cut into pieces
  • Potato starch or cornstarch — ¾ cup
  • Egg white — 1
  • Rice wine or mirin — 1 tbsp
  • Gochujang (Korean chili paste) — 3 tbsp
  • Honey or corn syrup — 3 tbsp
  • Brown sugar — 1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce — 1 tbsp
  • Minced garlic & ginger — 1 tbsp each
  • Toasted sesame oil & sesame seeds
  • Oil for deep frying

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Season chicken pieces with salt, black pepper, grated ginger, and rice wine. Toss with 1 egg white and coat thoroughly in potato starch.
  2. In a saucepan, combine gochujang, honey, brown sugar, soy sauce, minced garlic, and 2 tbsp water. Simmer on low heat for 3-4 minutes until thick and glossy; stir in sesame oil.
  3. Heat frying oil to 175°C (350°F). Fry chicken in batches for 5-6 minutes. Remove and drain on a wire rack for 5 minutes.
  4. Increase oil temperature to 190°C (375°F). Fry chicken a second time for 2-3 minutes until shatteringly crisp and golden.
  5. Transfer piping hot crispy chicken directly into a large bowl.
  6. Pour the warm sticky gochujang glaze over the chicken and toss quickly until every piece is evenly coated.
  7. Scatter toasted sesame seeds and chopped scallions over top and serve hot.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
