Dessert • 20 min • Easy

Mango Cardamom Kulfi

Creamy traditional frozen dessert made with ripe Alphonso mango puree, condensed milk, and crushed pistachios.

Ingredients

  • Sweet mango pulp — 1½ cups
  • Sweetened condensed milk — 1 can (400g)
  • Heavy whipping cream — 1 cup
  • Evaporated milk — 1 cup
  • Cardamom powder — ½ tsp
  • Crushed pistachios — 3 tbsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. In a blender, combine mango pulp, condensed milk, heavy cream, evaporated milk, and cardamom powder.
  2. Blend on high for 60 seconds until creamy and frothy.
  3. Stir in 2 tbsp of finely crushed pistachios.
  4. Pour mixture into traditional kulfi molds or popsicle containers. Insert wooden sticks.
  5. Freeze for at least 6-8 hours until completely set.
  6. Unmold by running warm water over the outside of the molds for 10 seconds.
  7. Garnish with remaining pistachios and serve frozen.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
