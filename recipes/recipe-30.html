Chicken • 40 min • Medium

Murgh Makhani (Butter Chicken)

Tender tandoori chicken pieces simmered in a velvety, buttery tomato and cashew cream gravy.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken thighs — 600g diced
  • Fresh tomato puree — 2 cups
  • Butter — 4 tbsp
  • Heavy cream — ½ cup
  • Cashew paste — 2 tbsp
  • Kasoori methi (dried fenugreek) — 1 tbsp
  • Honey or sugar — 1 tsp
  • Garam masala & Kashmiri chili powder — 1 tsp each

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, lemon juice, chili powder, and ginger garlic for 30 minutes. Pan sear until charred and cooked.
  2. In a saucepan, melt 2 tbsp butter, add whole spices and tomato puree with cashew paste. Simmer for 15 minutes until thick.
  3. Blend tomato sauce until silkily smooth, strain back into pan.
  4. Add cooked chicken pieces, remaining butter, heavy cream, honey, and garam masala.
  5. Simmer on low heat for 7-8 minutes until sauce is glossy.
  6. Crush kasoori methi between your palms and stir into curry. Garnish with a swirl of cream and serve with garlic naan.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
