Chicken • 40 min • Medium

Spicy Peri Peri Flame-Grilled Chicken

Fiery South African-Portuguese style grilled chicken basted in homemade African bird's eye chili, garlic, and citrus sauce.

Ingredients

  • Chicken leg quarters (with deep slashes) — 4 pieces
  • Bird's eye red chilies or red jalapeños — 4-5
  • Garlic cloves — 6
  • Fresh lemon juice & zest — 1 lemon
  • Smoked paprika & oregano — 1 tbsp each
  • Olive oil — ¼ cup
  • Red wine vinegar — 2 tbsp
  • Sea salt — 1½ tsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Blend chilies, garlic, lemon juice, zest, smoked paprika, oregano, vinegar, olive oil, and salt in a blender until smooth to create the fiery Peri Peri marinade.
  2. Rub marinade all over chicken leg quarters, getting deep inside the slashes. Marinate in refrigerator for at least 3 hours or overnight.
  3. Preheat barbecue grill or oven grill to medium-high heat (200°C / 400°F).
  4. Place chicken on the grill and cook for 30-35 minutes, turning every 7-8 minutes and basting generously with extra peri-peri sauce.
  5. Cook until skin is beautifully charred and crispy, and chicken juices run clear.
  6. Serve with grilled corn on the cob, spicy peri-peri salted fries, and lemon wedges.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
