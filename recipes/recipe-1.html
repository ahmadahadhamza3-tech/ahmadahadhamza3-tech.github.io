Pakistani • 60 min • Medium

Spicy Chicken Biryani

Fragrant basmati rice, tender chicken and bold Pakistani spices.

Ingredients

  • Chicken — 500g
  • Basmati rice — 500g
  • Onions — 2
  • Tomatoes — 2
  • Yogurt — 1 cup
  • Biryani masala — 2 tbsp
  • Ginger garlic paste — 1 tbsp
  • Oil — 4 tbsp

Cooking time

60 min

Difficulty

Medium

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Soak the rice for 20 minutes.
  2. Fry sliced onions until golden.
  3. Add chicken, tomatoes, yogurt and spices; cook until tender.
  4. Parboil the rice separately.
  5. Layer rice over the chicken mixture.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat for 15 minutes.
  7. Gently mix and serve hot with raita.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
