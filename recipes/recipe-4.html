Dessert • 50 min • Medium

Chocolate Cake

Soft, rich chocolate cake for birthdays, tea time or dessert cravings.

Ingredients

  • Flour — 1½ cups
  • Cocoa powder — ½ cup
  • Sugar — 1 cup
  • Eggs — 2
  • Milk — 1 cup
  • Butter — 100g
  • Baking powder — 1 tsp

Cooking time

50 min

Difficulty

Medium

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix flour, cocoa and baking powder.
  3. Beat eggs, sugar and butter.
  4. Add milk and dry ingredients.
  5. Pour into a greased pan.
  6. Bake for 35–40 minutes and cool before serving.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
