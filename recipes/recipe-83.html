Chicken • 30 min • Easy

Chicken Shawarma Wrap

Tender spiced chicken tucked into a soft flatbread with crunchy vegetables and a creamy garlic-yogurt sauce.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken — 400g, thinly sliced
  • Plain yogurt — 3 tbsp
  • Lemon juice — 1 tbsp
  • Garlic — 2 cloves, minced
  • Ground cumin — 1 tsp
  • Paprika — 1 tsp
  • Ground coriander — ½ tsp
  • Olive oil — 1 tbsp
  • Flatbreads or tortillas — 4
  • Lettuce and cucumber — sliced
  • Tomato — 1, sliced
  • Yogurt-garlic sauce — ½ cup

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, cumin, paprika, coriander, oil, and a little salt.
  2. Coat chicken in the marinade and leave for at least 15 minutes in the refrigerator.
  3. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat and cook chicken until browned and fully cooked to 74°C (165°F).
  4. Warm the flatbreads in a dry pan.
  5. Spread yogurt-garlic sauce over each flatbread and add chicken, lettuce, cucumber, and tomato.
  6. Roll tightly, slice in half, and serve warm.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting. Cooking times can vary with equipment and ingredient size; make sure meat and poultry are fully cooked before serving.
