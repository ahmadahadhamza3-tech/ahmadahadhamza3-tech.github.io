Creamy Tomato Basil Soup

A comforting tomato soup blended until silky and finished with fragrant basil and a little cream.

Ingredients

  • Ripe tomatoes — 700g, chopped
  • Onion — 1, diced
  • Garlic — 3 cloves, minced
  • Vegetable stock — 2 cups
  • Olive oil — 1 tbsp
  • Tomato paste — 1 tbsp
  • Fresh basil — a small handful
  • Cream — ¼ cup, optional
  • Salt and black pepper — to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a pot and sauté onion for 4–5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add garlic and tomato paste; cook for 1 minute.
  3. Stir in tomatoes and vegetable stock, then simmer for 15–20 minutes until tomatoes are soft.
  4. Blend carefully until smooth, working in batches if needed.
  5. Return soup to low heat, stir in basil and optional cream, and season to taste.
  6. Warm gently without boiling and serve with toast or grilled cheese.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting. Cooking times can vary with equipment and ingredient size; make sure meat and poultry are fully cooked before serving.
