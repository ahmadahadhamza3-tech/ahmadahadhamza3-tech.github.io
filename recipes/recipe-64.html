Creamy Garlic Chicken Alfredo Penne

Golden pan-seared garlic chicken breast tossed with al dente penne pasta in a luscious homemade heavy cream and aged Parmigiano-Reggiano sauce.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken breasts — 400g sliced into cutlets
  • Penne pasta — 300g
  • Butter — 3 tbsp
  • Garlic cloves — 5 minced
  • Heavy whipping cream — 1½ cups
  • Freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese — 1 cup
  • Italian seasoning & garlic powder — 1 tsp each
  • Fresh flat-leaf parsley — 2 tbsp chopped
  • Sea salt & freshly cracked black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil penne pasta in heavily salted water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water, then drain.
  2. Season chicken cutlets with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  3. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken for 4-5 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through. Remove to a cutting board and slice into strips.
  4. In the same skillet, melt remaining 2 tbsp butter over medium heat. Sauté minced garlic for 1 minute until fragrant.
  5. Pour in heavy whipping cream and bring to a gentle simmer for 3 minutes.
  6. Reduce heat to low and gradually whisk in grated Parmesan cheese until melted and velvety smooth.
  7. Toss the drained penne and sliced chicken into the sauce, adding a splash of reserved pasta water if needed to coat silkily.
  8. Garnish with freshly cracked black pepper and chopped parsley. Serve immediately.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
