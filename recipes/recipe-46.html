Crispy Golden Belgian Waffles

Crispy on the outside, fluffy on the inside Belgian waffles with deep pockets for pure maple syrup and berries.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour — 2 cups
  • Baking powder — 1 tbsp + Sugar — 2 tbsp
  • Eggs — 2 separated (whites whipped to stiff peaks)
  • Milk — 1¾ cups
  • Melted butter — ½ cup
  • Vanilla extract — 1 tsp
  • Pure maple syrup & fresh berries

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk milk, melted butter, egg yolks, and vanilla. Stir into dry ingredients until combined.
  3. Gently fold whipped egg whites into the batter with a spatula for cloud-like fluffiness.
  4. Preheat and grease waffle iron. Pour batter and cook until golden brown and steam stops (4-5 minutes).
  5. Serve hot topped with fresh strawberries, blueberries, a pat of butter, and maple syrup.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
