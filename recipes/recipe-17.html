Loaded Cheesy Beef Nachos

Crispy corn tortilla chips piled high with seasoned beef, melted cheddar, jalapeños, and guacamole.

Ingredients

  • Tortilla chips — 1 large bag
  • Ground beef — 300g seasoned with taco spices
  • Cheddar cheese — 2 cups shredded
  • Pickled jalapeños — ¼ cup
  • Pico de gallo salsa — ½ cup
  • Guacamole — ½ cup
  • Sour cream — 3 tbsp

Cooking time

20 min

Difficulty

Easy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Brown ground beef with taco seasoning in a skillet until fully cooked.
  3. Arrange tortilla chips on a large baking sheet in an even layer.
  4. Top with seasoned beef, black beans, jalapeño slices, and shredded cheddar cheese.
  5. Bake for 8-10 minutes until cheese is bubbly and melted.
  6. Top with fresh salsa, dollops of guacamole, sour cream, and serve immediately.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
