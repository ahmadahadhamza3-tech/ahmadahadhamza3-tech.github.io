Sticky Honey Garlic Chicken Thighs

Tender, caramelized chicken thighs coated in a sweet, sticky garlic and soy glaze with sesame seeds and green onions.

Ingredients

  • Boneless skinless chicken thighs — 600g
  • Honey — ⅓ cup
  • Low-sodium soy sauce — ¼ cup
  • Garlic cloves — 6 minced
  • Apple cider vinegar — 1 tbsp
  • Cornstarch — 1 tsp dissolved in 1 tbsp water
  • Toasted sesame oil — 1 tsp
  • Toasted sesame seeds & sliced scallions for garnish

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Season chicken thighs lightly with salt and black pepper.
  2. In a bowl, whisk honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, apple cider vinegar, sesame oil, and cornstarch slurry.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp vegetable oil in a heavy skillet or cast iron over medium-high heat.
  4. Add chicken thighs and sear for 5-6 minutes per side until browned and cooked through.
  5. Pour the honey garlic sauce directly into the hot skillet with the chicken.
  6. Simmer for 2-3 minutes, basting chicken continuously as the sauce bubbles into a thick, glossy, sticky glaze.
  7. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Serve over fragrant jasmine rice.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
