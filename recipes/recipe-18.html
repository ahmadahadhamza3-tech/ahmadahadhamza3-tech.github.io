Crispy French Fries & Secret Sauce

Double-fried golden shoestring potatoes seasoned with sea salt and smoked paprika served with tangy dip.

Ingredients

  • Russet potatoes — 4 large
  • Vegetable oil — for deep frying
  • Sea salt — 1 tsp
  • Smoked paprika — ½ tsp
  • Garlic powder — ½ tsp
  • Mayonnaise — 3 tbsp
  • Ketchup — 2 tbsp
  • Sweet pickle relish — 1 tbsp

Cooking time

25 min

Difficulty

Easy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Peel and cut potatoes into uniform thin fries (¼ inch).
  2. Soak cut fries in ice-cold water for 30 minutes to remove excess starch, then pat completely dry.
  3. Heat frying oil to 160°C (320°F) and fry potatoes for 4-5 minutes until soft but pale. Remove and drain.
  4. Increase oil temperature to 190°C (375°F) and fry again for 3-4 minutes until deeply golden and extra crispy.
  5. Toss hot fries in a bowl with sea salt, garlic powder, and smoked paprika.
  6. Whisk mayo, ketchup, relish, and pepper to make the signature burger sauce.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
