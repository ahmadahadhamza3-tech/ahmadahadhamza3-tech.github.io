Smoky Sizzling Chicken Fajitas

Sizzling cast-iron strips of spiced chicken breast, colorful bell peppers, and caramelized onions served in a smoking skillet with warm tortillas and guacamole.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — 500g sliced into thin strips
  • Bell peppers (red, green, yellow) — 3 sliced into strips
  • Red onion — 1 large sliced
  • Fajita spice blend (chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic, oregano) — 2 tbsp
  • Fresh lime juice — 2 tbsp
  • Olive oil — 2 tbsp
  • Warm flour tortillas, guacamole, salsa, and sour cream for serving

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Toss chicken strips with olive oil, lime juice, and fajita seasoning in a bowl. Rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat a heavy cast-iron skillet over high heat until smoking hot.
  3. Add 1 tbsp oil and sear chicken strips in a single layer for 5-6 minutes until nicely charred and cooked through. Remove chicken.
  4. Add sliced bell peppers and onions to the smoking skillet; stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until blistered with charred edges yet crisp-tender.
  5. Toss chicken back into the skillet with the sizzling peppers and onions.
  6. Squeeze fresh lime juice over the pan for signature table sizzle and serve immediately with warm tortillas, guacamole, salsa, and sour cream.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
