Chicken • 25 min • Easy

Japanese Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowl (Donburi)

Juicy pan-seared chicken thighs glazed in homemade sweet soy teriyaki sauce over steamed rice with steamed broccoli and toasted sesame.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken thighs — 450g
  • Soy sauce — 3 tbsp
  • Mirin (Japanese sweet rice wine) — 2 tbsp
  • Sake or water — 1 tbsp
  • Brown sugar or honey — 1½ tbsp
  • Fresh ginger — 1 tsp grated
  • Garlic clove — 1 minced
  • Steamed Jasmine or Calrose rice — 2 cups
  • Steamed broccoli florets & toasted sesame seeds

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Whisk soy sauce, mirin, sake (or water), brown sugar, and grated ginger in a small bowl until sugar dissolves.
  2. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Place chicken thighs smooth-side down and cook for 5 minutes until crispy and golden.
  3. Flip chicken and cook for another 4 minutes until cooked through. Drain excess fat from the pan.
  4. Pour the teriyaki sauce mixture into the pan around the chicken.
  5. Let the sauce bubble and reduce for 2-3 minutes, turning chicken repeatedly until it is thickly coated in a glossy caramel glaze.
  6. Remove chicken to a cutting board and slice into bite-sized strips.
  7. Fill bowls with warm steamed rice, arrange sliced teriyaki chicken and steamed broccoli, and spoon extra warm teriyaki glaze on top with sesame seeds.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
