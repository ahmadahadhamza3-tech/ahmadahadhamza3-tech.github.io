Loaded Supreme Nachos Grande

Piled-high stone-ground crispy tortilla chips smothered in molten queso cheese sauce, seasoned ground beef, black beans, pico de gallo, and jalapeño slices.

Ingredients

  • Stone-ground restaurant style tortilla chips — 1 large bag (350g)
  • Ground beef or seasoned black beans — 300g
  • Taco seasoning — 2 tbsp
  • Molten cheddar queso sauce — 1 cup
  • Shredded Mexican blend cheese (Monterey Jack & Cheddar) — 2 cups
  • Pickled jalapeño slices — ¼ cup
  • Fresh pico de gallo (diced tomatoes, onions, cilantro, lime) — ½ cup
  • Guacamole & sour cream — ½ cup each

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. In a skillet over medium-high heat, brown the ground beef with taco seasoning and ¼ cup water until cooked and savory.
  2. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F) or set oven grill to high.
  3. Spread a generous layer of tortilla chips across a wide parchment-lined baking sheet.
  4. Evenly scatter shredded cheese, seasoned ground beef, and black beans over the chips. Add a second layer of chips and cheese on top.
  5. Bake in oven for 6-8 minutes until shredded cheese is bubbling and melted.
  6. Drizzle warm melted queso sauce over the entire hot platter.
  7. Top generously with fresh pico de gallo, pickled jalapeño rings, dollops of cool sour cream, and fresh guacamole. Serve right from the tray.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
