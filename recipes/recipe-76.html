Nutella & Strawberry Stuffed French Toast

Thick-cut golden brioche slices filled with rich hazelnut Nutella spread and fresh strawberries, soaked in spiced vanilla custard and pan-toasted in butter.

Ingredients

  • Thick-cut brioche or challah bread — 4 slices
  • Nutella hazelnut spread — 4 tbsp
  • Fresh strawberries — 1 cup thinly sliced
  • Large eggs — 2
  • Whole milk or half-and-half — ½ cup
  • Pure vanilla extract — 1 tsp
  • Ground cinnamon — ½ tsp
  • Butter for cooking — 2 tbsp
  • Powdered sugar & pure maple syrup for serving

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Spread 2 tablespoons of Nutella generously onto two slices of brioche.
  2. Layer thinly sliced fresh strawberries over the Nutella, then top with the remaining brioche slices to form sandwiches. Press edges gently.
  3. In a wide shallow dish, whisk eggs, milk, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt into a smooth custard.
  4. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat.
  5. Dip each sandwich into the custard mixture for 10-15 seconds per side, allowing the bread to absorb without getting soggy.
  6. Place sandwiches in the hot pan and cook for 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown and crispy on the outside, and Nutella is melty inside.
  7. Cut diagonally, dust with powdered sugar, and serve with fresh strawberries and warm maple syrup.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
