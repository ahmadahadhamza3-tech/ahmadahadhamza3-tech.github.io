Lemon Herb Grilled Salmon & Asparagus

Pan-seared wild salmon fillets seasoned with garlic, dill, and lemon served alongside crisp tender asparagus.

Ingredients

  • Fresh salmon fillets — 2 (200g each)
  • Fresh asparagus spears — 1 bunch trimmed
  • Olive oil — 2 tbsp
  • Garlic cloves — 3 minced
  • Lemon juice & zest — 1 lemon
  • Fresh dill — 2 tbsp chopped
  • Sea salt & crushed black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Pat salmon fillets dry and season both sides with salt, pepper, garlic, and fresh dill.
  2. Toss trimmed asparagus in 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Heat a heavy skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat with remaining olive oil.
  4. Place salmon skin-side down and cook undisturbed for 4-5 minutes until skin is crispy.
  5. Flip salmon and add asparagus to the side of the pan. Cook for another 3-4 minutes.
  6. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over salmon and asparagus.
  7. Serve immediately with lemon wedges and a light herb drizzle.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
