Zinger Crunch Chicken Burger

Super crunchy buttermilk battered chicken breast with spicy secret mayo, cheddar, and crisp lettuce on toasted brioche.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast fillets — 2
  • Brioche burger buns — 2
  • Buttermilk — 1 cup + 1 egg
  • Flour + cornstarch blend — 1½ cups
  • Spicy seasoning (paprika, cayenne, garlic, salt) — 2 tbsp
  • Spicy mayonnaise — 3 tbsp
  • Pickle chips — 6 slices
  • Crisp iceberg lettuce

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Pound chicken breasts to even ½-inch thickness and marinate in spiced buttermilk for 30 minutes.
  2. Whisk flour, cornstarch, cayenne, paprika, garlic powder, and salt. Drizzle 2 tbsp buttermilk into flour to create crunchy flakes.
  3. Dredge chicken into flour mix, dip back into buttermilk, then press firmly into flour mix.
  4. Deep fry at 175°C (350°F) for 6-7 minutes until ultra-crispy, golden, and cooked through (internal 74°C / 165°F).
  5. Toast brioche buns in butter until golden.
  6. Spread spicy mayo on both buns, layer crunchy lettuce, pickles, and the sizzling hot crunchy chicken fillet.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
