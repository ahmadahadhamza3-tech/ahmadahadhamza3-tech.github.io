Traditional Cast-Iron Shakshuka with Feta

Farm-fresh organic eggs gently poached in a bubbling cast-iron skillet of spiced tomato, roasted red bell pepper, and cumin sauce topped with crumbled feta.

Ingredients

  • Large eggs — 4-5
  • Ripe crushed plum tomatoes (canned or fresh) — 1 can (400g)
  • Red bell pepper — 1 diced
  • Yellow onion — 1 diced
  • Garlic cloves — 3 minced
  • Ground cumin & smoked paprika — 1 tsp each
  • Crushed red pepper flakes — ½ tsp
  • Crumbled Greek feta cheese — ⅓ cup
  • Fresh cilantro and parsley — 2 tbsp chopped
  • Olive oil — 2 tbsp
  • Crusty sourdough bread for dipping

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a heavy cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Sauté diced onion and bell pepper for 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and chili flakes; sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Pour in crushed tomatoes and season with salt and pepper. Simmer gently for 10 minutes until sauce thickens.
  4. Use the back of a large spoon to make 4-5 shallow wells in the bubbling sauce.
  5. Crack an egg carefully into each well.
  6. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover with a lid, and cook for 5-7 minutes until egg whites are just set but yolks remain soft and runny.
  7. Remove from heat, scatter crumbled feta cheese, fresh cilantro, and parsley over the skillet.
  8. Serve immediately straight from the cast iron with warm crusty bread.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
