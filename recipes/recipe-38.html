Dessert • 75 min • Hard

Classic New York Cheesecake

Silky smooth, rich baked cheesecake on a buttery graham cracker crust with berry coulis.

Ingredients

  • Cream cheese (softened) — 600g
  • Graham cracker / digestive biscuits — 180g crushed
  • Melted butter — 4 tbsp
  • Sugar — 1 cup
  • Sour cream — ½ cup
  • Eggs — 3
  • Vanilla extract — 1 tbsp
  • Lemon zest — 1 tsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 160°C (325°F). Mix biscuit crumbs with melted butter and press firmly into an 8-inch springform pan. Bake crust for 10 min.
  2. Beat cream cheese and sugar on low speed until smooth and lump-free.
  3. Add sour cream, vanilla, lemon zest, and beat in eggs one at a time on low speed.
  4. Pour batter over crust. Place springform pan in a larger roasting pan with warm water (water bath).
  5. Bake for 55-60 minutes until edges are set but center has a slight jiggle.
  6. Turn off oven, prop door open slightly, and let cool for 1 hour inside oven.
  7. Refrigerate for at least 6 hours before slicing. Serve with fresh raspberry coulis.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
