Dessert • 30 min • Easy

Classic Italian Tiramisu

Velvety espresso-soaked Savoiardi ladyfingers layered with rich mascarpone zabaglione cream and generously dusted with dark Dutch cocoa powder.

Ingredients

  • Savoiardi Italian ladyfingers — 24-30 biscuits
  • Mascarpone cheese (chilled) — 500g
  • Fresh egg yolks — 4
  • Granulated sugar — ½ cup
  • Heavy whipping cream — 1 cup
  • Strong brewed espresso coffee (cooled) — 1½ cups
  • Marsala wine or coffee liqueur (optional) — 2 tbsp
  • Pure vanilla extract — 1 tsp
  • Dutch unsweetened cocoa powder for dusting

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. In a heatproof bowl set over simmering water (double boiler), whisk egg yolks and sugar for 5 minutes until pale, thick, and sugar dissolves. Remove and cool completely.
  2. In a separate bowl, beat mascarpone cheese and vanilla extract until smooth, then gently fold into the cooled egg mixture.
  3. Whip heavy cream in another bowl until stiff peaks form. Gently fold whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture until light and airy.
  4. Mix cooled espresso and Marsala wine in a wide shallow dish.
  5. Quickly dip each ladyfinger biscuit into the espresso for 1-2 seconds per side (do not oversaturate) and arrange in a single layer at the bottom of an 8x8 inch dish.
  6. Spread half of the mascarpone cream evenly over the soaked biscuits.
  7. Add a second layer of dipped ladyfingers, then cover with the remaining mascarpone cream, smoothing the top.
  8. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours (preferably overnight). Dust generously with dark cocoa powder right before slicing.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
