Chicken • 40 min • Medium

Shahi Mughlai White Chicken Korma

A royal Mughlai white curry made with tender chicken braised in a luxurious cashew-almond nut cream, whisked yogurt, white pepper, and fragrant kewra water.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces — 750g
  • Thick yogurt — 1 cup whisked
  • Blanched almonds & cashews paste — ¼ cup
  • White onion paste — ½ cup
  • Ginger garlic paste — 2 tbsp
  • Whole green cardamom & cloves — 4 each
  • White pepper powder — 1 tsp
  • Pure Desi Ghee — 4 tbsp
  • Kewra water — 1 tsp
  • Slivered almonds & slit green chilies for garnish

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat ghee in a heavy pot and add green cardamoms, cloves, and bay leaf until aromatic.
  2. Add ginger garlic paste and chicken pieces; sauté on medium heat for 6-8 minutes until chicken turns opaque.
  3. Lower the heat and add whisked yogurt, white onion paste, and white pepper. Stir continuously for 3 minutes to prevent curdling.
  4. Add the cashew and almond nut paste with ½ cup warm water. Mix well.
  5. Cover with a tight lid and simmer on low heat (Dum) for 20 minutes until chicken is tender and fragrant ghee rises to the surface.
  6. Stir in a drop of kewra water and green chili slits.
  7. Garnish with slivered almonds and serve with rogheni naan or sheermal.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
