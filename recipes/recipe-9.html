Pakistani • 15 min • Easy

Lahori Chana Chaat

Tangy, spicy chickpea street-food salad with potatoes, tamarind and onion.

Ingredients

  • Boiled chickpeas — 2 cups
  • Boiled potatoes — 2 diced
  • Onion — 1 finely chopped
  • Tomato — 1 chopped
  • Green chili — 2
  • Tamarind chutney — 3 tbsp
  • Chaat masala — 1 tsp

Cooking time

15 min

Difficulty

Easy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Combine boiled chickpeas and diced potatoes in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add chopped onions, tomatoes, and fresh green chilies.
  3. Drizzle tangy tamarind chutney and sprinkle chaat masala.
  4. Toss everything together and garnish with fresh coriander.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
