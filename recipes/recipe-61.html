Pakistani • 40 min • Medium

Peshawari Shinwari Lamb Karahi (Namak Mandi)

Legendary Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Namak Mandi style lamb karahi cooked simply in animal fat or desi ghee with ripe tomatoes, coarse black pepper, and whole green chilies.

Ingredients

  • Tender lamb or goat cuts (bone-in) — 750g
  • Ripe red tomatoes — 5 halved
  • Desi Ghee or lamb tallow (charbi) — ½ cup
  • Garlic paste (in water) — 2 tbsp
  • Fresh ginger julienned — 2 tbsp
  • Whole green chilies (slit) — 6
  • Coarsely crushed black pepper — 1½ tbsp
  • Rock salt (namak) — 1 tsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat desi ghee or lamb fat in a heavy cast-iron wok (karahi) over high heat until sizzling.
  2. Add lamb pieces and salt; sear on high flame for 6-8 minutes until the meat turns golden-brown on all edges.
  3. Pour in garlic water and ½ cup warm water. Cover with a tight lid and simmer on medium heat for 20-25 minutes until lamb is tender.
  4. Arrange halved tomatoes over the lamb cut-side down. Cover for 5 minutes so steam softens the skins.
  5. Use tongs to easily peel off and discard tomato skins.
  6. Mash the soft tomatoes into the meat using a wooden spatula over high flame, sautéing vigorously (Bhunai) until the tomato juices reduce into a rich, glossy gravy that coats the meat.
  7. Toss in slit green chilies and freshly crushed black pepper during the final 3 minutes of high-heat stir-frying.
  8. Garnish with fresh julienned ginger and serve sizzling hot directly in the karahi with hot tandoori naan and green tea.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
