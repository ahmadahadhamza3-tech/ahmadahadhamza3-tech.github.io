Classic Cheeseburger

Juicy homemade burger with melted cheese and fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Burger buns — 2
  • Beef patties — 2
  • Cheese slices — 2
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Mayonnaise
  • Ketchup

Cooking time

25 min

Difficulty

Easy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook the beef patties on a hot pan.
  2. Add cheese during the final minute.
  3. Toast the buns.
  4. Spread sauces and add vegetables.
  5. Place the patty inside and serve.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
