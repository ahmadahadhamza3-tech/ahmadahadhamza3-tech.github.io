Dessert • 40 min • Medium

Royal Shahi Saffron Zarda Sweet Rice

Celebratory Mughlai sweet basmati rice infused with fragrant saffron strands, green cardamom, desi ghee, candied fruits, raisins, and roasted nuts.

Ingredients

  • Aged sella basmati rice — 2 cups (soaked for 2 hours)
  • Sugar — 1¾ cups
  • Pure Desi Ghee — ½ cup
  • Saffron strands — 1 generous pinch steeped in 2 tbsp warm milk
  • Whole green cardamom pods — 6
  • Cloves — 4
  • Kewra water — 1 tsp
  • Ashrafi / Murabba (candied fruit / tutty fruity) — ½ cup
  • Almonds, pistachios & cashews (slivered) — ½ cup
  • Khoya / Mawa (evaporated milk solids) or mini gulab jamun for garnish

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil 6 cups of water in a large pot with green cardamoms, cloves, and saffron milk until fragrant and bright golden-yellow.
  2. Add soaked and drained sella basmati rice. Boil until rice is 85-90% cooked through (firm core). Drain water completely in a colander.
  3. In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat desi ghee on medium flame. Lightly fry slivered almonds, pistachios, cashews, and raisins for 1 minute until fragrant. Remove half for garnish.
  4. In the remaining ghee, add sugar and ½ cup water; simmer for 2-3 minutes until sugar dissolves into a light syrup.
  5. Gently slide the drained warm rice into the sugar syrup, tossing very lightly with a flat wooden spoon to coat without breaking rice grains.
  6. Cook on medium-high heat until most of the syrup bubbles down. Sprinkle kewra water and candied fruits on top.
  7. Cover with a tight lid sealed with a clean kitchen towel (Dum). Place on lowest heat over an iron tawa for 15 minutes.
  8. Gently fluff the golden rice grains, top with crumbled khoya and roasted nuts, and serve warm.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
