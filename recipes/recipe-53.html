Chicken • 45 min • Medium

Japanese Crispy Chicken Katsu Curry

Crunchy Japanese panko-breaded chicken cutlet sliced over steamed short-grain rice with rich, savory Japanese curry sauce.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken thighs or breasts — 2
  • Japanese panko breadcrumbs — 1½ cups
  • Japanese curry roux cubes (e.g., S&B Golden Curry) — 3 cubes
  • Carrot — 1 chopped
  • Yellow onion — 1 diced
  • Potato — 1 peeled & cubed
  • Chicken stock or water — 3 cups
  • Steamed Japanese sushi rice — 3 cups
  • Oil for deep frying

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. In a pot, heat 1 tbsp oil and sauté diced onion, carrot, and potato for 5 minutes.
  2. Add chicken stock, bring to a boil, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until vegetables are tender.
  3. Turn off heat, break in Japanese curry roux cubes, and stir until completely dissolved. Simmer on low for 5 minutes until thick and glossy.
  4. Pound chicken lightly, season with salt and white pepper, dredge in flour, egg, and coat thoroughly with panko breadcrumbs.
  5. Deep fry chicken in hot oil (175°C / 350°F) for 5-6 minutes until crispy and golden. Drain on a wire rack for 2 minutes, then slice into strips.
  6. Serve steamed short-grain rice on one side of a wide bowl, arrange sliced katsu next to it, and ladle hot curry sauce generously around.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
