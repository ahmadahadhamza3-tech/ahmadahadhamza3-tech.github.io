Mediterranean Quinoa Tabbouleh Superfood Bowl

A crisp superfood bowl packed with fluffy cooked quinoa, mountains of finely chopped Italian parsley, fresh mint, Persian cucumbers, heirloom tomatoes, and extra virgin olive oil.

Ingredients

  • Tri-color or white quinoa — 1 cup (rinsed & cooked fluffy)
  • Fresh flat-leaf parsley — 2 large bunches finely chopped
  • Fresh mint leaves — ½ cup finely chopped
  • Persian cucumbers — 3 diced small
  • Ripe heirloom tomatoes — 2 seeded & diced small
  • Green onions (scallions) — 4 thinly sliced
  • Extra virgin cold-pressed olive oil — ⅓ cup
  • Fresh lemon juice — ¼ cup
  • Ground sumac — 1 tsp
  • Himalayan pink salt & cracked black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook rinsed quinoa in 2 cups of water with a pinch of salt for 15 minutes until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and cool completely.
  2. Finely chop washed and thoroughly dried parsley and mint leaves.
  3. In a large wooden salad bowl, combine cooled quinoa, chopped parsley, mint, diced cucumbers, tomatoes, and scallions.
  4. In a small mason jar, shake together olive oil, freshly squeezed lemon juice, ground sumac, sea salt, and black pepper until emulsified.
  5. Drizzle the citrus dressing over the salad and toss gently to coat every grain and herb.
  6. Chill for 15 minutes before serving to let flavors marry. Enjoy as a wholesome energizing meal or side.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
