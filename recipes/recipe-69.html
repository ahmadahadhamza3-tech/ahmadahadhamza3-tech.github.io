Crispy Nashville Hot Chicken Slider

Buttermilk-dunked crispy chicken thigh dipped in fiery cayenne-brown sugar chili oil, nestled on toasted brioche with sweet crinkle-cut pickles and creamy coleslaw.

Ingredients

  • Boneless skinless chicken thighs — 4 cut in half for sliders
  • Brioche slider buns — 4 toasted
  • Buttermilk — 1 cup
  • All-purpose flour — 1½ cups
  • Cornstarch — ¼ cup
  • Cayenne pepper — 2 tbsp
  • Dark brown sugar — 1½ tbsp
  • Smoked paprika, garlic powder & chili powder — 1 tsp each
  • Crispy crinkle-cut dill pickle chips
  • Creamy cool cabbage slaw — 1 cup
  • Frying oil

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken pieces in buttermilk seasoned with hot sauce and salt for 30 minutes.
  2. Whisk flour, cornstarch, salt, and paprika in a shallow bowl. Dredge chicken in flour, dip back into buttermilk, and dredge in flour again.
  3. Heat frying oil to 175°C (350°F). Fry chicken pieces for 6-7 minutes until extra crunchy and golden. Drain on wire rack.
  4. Ladle ½ cup of the piping hot frying oil into a heatproof bowl. Whisk in cayenne pepper, brown sugar, paprika, garlic powder, and a pinch of salt to form the Nashville hot chili oil.
  5. Immediately brush or dunk each crispy chicken piece into the hot chili oil.
  6. Toast brioche slider buns with butter on a skillet.
  7. Assemble sliders: bottom bun, creamy slaw, hot dipped chicken, crinkle pickle chips, and top bun. Enjoy hot!

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
