Chicken Tikka

Smoky, spicy grilled chicken with a delicious charred finish.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces — 500g
  • Yogurt — ½ cup
  • Tikka masala — 2 tbsp
  • Lemon juice — 2 tbsp
  • Ginger garlic paste — 1 tbsp
  • Oil — 2 tbsp

Cooking time

40 min

Difficulty

Medium

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix yogurt, tikka masala, lemon and ginger garlic.
  2. Marinate chicken for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Thread onto skewers.
  4. Grill until cooked and lightly charred.
  5. Brush with butter or oil and serve hot.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
