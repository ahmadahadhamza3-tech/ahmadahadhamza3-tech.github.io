Pan-Seared Citrus Herb Salmon with Asparagus

Omega-3 rich wild salmon fillet pan-seared with crispy skin, basted in lemon-dill garlic olive oil alongside tender blistered asparagus spears.

Ingredients

  • Wild salmon fillets (skin-on) — 2 (approx 180g each)
  • Fresh asparagus spears (woody ends trimmed) — 1 bunch
  • Extra virgin olive oil — 2 tbsp
  • Unsalted butter or ghee — 1 tbsp
  • Garlic cloves — 3 minced
  • Fresh dill — 2 tbsp chopped
  • Fresh lemon juice & zest — 1 lemon
  • Flaky sea salt & coarse black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Pat salmon fillets thoroughly dry with paper towels. Season both sides with flaky sea salt and cracked black pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil in a stainless steel or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  3. Place salmon fillets skin-side down and gently press with a spatula for 10 seconds to ensure even contact. Cook undisturbed for 5 minutes until skin is crispy and golden.
  4. Flip salmon carefully and toss the trimmed asparagus spears directly into the pan alongside the fish.
  5. Add butter, minced garlic, lemon zest, and lemon juice.
  6. Baste the top of the salmon continuously with the bubbling lemon-garlic butter for 2-3 minutes while asparagus blisters and becomes crisp-tender.
  7. Transfer to warm plates, sprinkle with fresh dill, and serve immediately with lemon wedges.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
