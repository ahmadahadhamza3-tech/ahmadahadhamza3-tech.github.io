Traditional Balochi Sajji with Spiced Rice

Authentic Balochistan slow-roasted whole leg of lamb or chicken infused with rock salt, green papaya, and special Sajji masala served over fragrant spiced rice.

Ingredients

  • Whole chicken or lamb leg (with deep slits) — 1.2kg
  • Rock salt (namak) — 1½ tbsp
  • Raw green papaya paste or vinegar — 2 tbsp
  • Special Sajji masala (coriander, cumin, amchur, carom, black pepper) — 2 tbsp
  • Fresh lemon juice — 3 tbsp
  • Desi Ghee or oil — 3 tbsp for basting
  • Aged basmati rice cooked with whole spices — 3 cups
  • Lemon wedges and chaat masala for serving

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Rub the chicken or lamb thoroughly inside and outside with rock salt, raw papaya paste, and lemon juice. Rest for 2 hours.
  2. Thread the meat onto large metal skewers (or place on a roasting rack over a baking pan).
  3. Slow roast over smoky coals or in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) for 45-50 minutes, rotating occasionally.
  4. As the meat roasts, baste with melted desi ghee and dust generously with the fragrant Sajji spice blend.
  5. Increase heat to 220°C (425°F) for the last 8 minutes to get an appetizing crisp, golden exterior while remaining juicy inside.
  6. Prepare aromatic basmati rice cooked with whole cardamom, cumin, and fried onions.
  7. Spread the hot spiced rice over a large ceremonial platter (Thaal), rest the sizzling carved Sajji on top, dust with more Sajji masala, and serve with fresh lemon wedges.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
