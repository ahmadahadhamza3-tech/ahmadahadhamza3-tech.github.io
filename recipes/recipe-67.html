Authentic Philly Cheesesteak Hoagie

Ultra-thin ribeye steak flash-seared on a smoking flat top with caramelized sweet onions and melted provolone & Cheez Whiz in a toasted Amoroso roll.

Ingredients

  • Ribeye steak (partially frozen & shaved paper-thin) — 450g
  • Hoagie or sub rolls — 2 split lengthwise
  • Yellow sweet onions — 2 thinly sliced
  • Provolone cheese slices — 4-6
  • Cheez Whiz or melted cheddar sauce — 3 tbsp
  • Butter & vegetable oil — 2 tbsp each
  • Salt & freshly cracked black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil and 1 tbsp butter on a large griddle or heavy skillet over medium heat. Sauté sliced onions until soft, golden, and caramelized (about 8-10 minutes). Push to the side.
  2. Turn the heat to high. Add remaining oil and place shaved ribeye directly onto the hot surface.
  3. Let it sizzle for 2 minutes, then use two spatulas to quickly chop and tear the meat as it browns.
  4. Season meat generously with salt and coarse black pepper. Mix the caramelized onions back into the beef.
  5. Shape the beef-onion mixture into a long rectangle matching the length of your hoagie roll.
  6. Lay provolone slices over the hot meat. Place the open-faced hoagie roll directly over the melting cheese to steam for 1 minute.
  7. Slide your long spatula underneath the entire stack, flip it into the roll, and drizzle with warm Cheez Whiz. Serve immediately.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
