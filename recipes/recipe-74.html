Thai Coconut Lentil Sweet Potato Curry

Nutrient-dense red lentils and roasted sweet potato chunks gently simmered in a coconut milk broth with fresh turmeric, baby spinach, and lime juice.

Ingredients

  • Red split lentils (masoor dal) — 1 cup rinsed
  • Sweet potatoes — 2 medium peeled & diced into bite-sized cubes
  • Coconut milk (unsweetened) — 1 can (400ml)
  • Vegetable stock — 2 cups
  • Yellow onion — 1 diced
  • Garlic — 4 cloves minced
  • Fresh ginger — 1 tbsp grated
  • Thai red curry paste or yellow curry powder — 2 tbsp
  • Baby spinach — 3 cups
  • Fresh lime juice — 2 tbsp
  • Coconut oil — 1 tbsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat coconut oil in a deep pot over medium heat. Sauté diced onion, minced garlic, and grated ginger for 3-4 minutes until softened.
  2. Stir in curry paste and cook for 1 minute until aromatic and fragrant.
  3. Add diced sweet potato cubes and rinsed red lentils, stirring to coat with the curry aromatics.
  4. Pour in vegetable broth and coconut milk. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low-medium.
  5. Cover and simmer gently for 20 minutes until sweet potatoes are fork-tender and lentils have melted into a creamy stew.
  6. Stir in fresh baby spinach and cook for 2 minutes until just wilted.
  7. Remove from heat, stir in fresh lime juice and season with salt. Ladle into bowls with brown rice or enjoy as a hearty soup.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
