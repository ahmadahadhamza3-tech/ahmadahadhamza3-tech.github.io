Sweet Potato & Black Bean Fiesta Bowl

Roasted cinnamon-cumin sweet potato cubes, spiced black beans, sweet corn, brown rice, and cilantro lime dressing.

Ingredients

  • Sweet potato — 1 large diced
  • Black beans — 1 cup cooked
  • Sweet corn kernels — ½ cup
  • Cooked brown rice — 1½ cups
  • Fresh cilantro — ¼ cup chopped
  • Lime juice — 2 tbsp
  • Olive oil — 1 tbsp
  • Cumin & chili powder — 1 tsp each

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Toss sweet potato cubes with olive oil, cumin, chili powder, and salt. Roast at 200°C (400°F) for 20 minutes until tender and caramelized.
  2. Warm black beans with a pinch of garlic powder and cumin.
  3. In a serving bowl, arrange warm brown rice, roasted sweet potatoes, black beans, and sweet corn.
  4. Whisk olive oil, fresh lime juice, salt, and chopped cilantro.
  5. Drizzle dressing over the bowl and garnish with avocado slices and lime.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
