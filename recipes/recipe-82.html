Pakistani • 35 min • Medium

Peshawari Chapli Kabab

Juicy, pan-fried Pashtun-style minced beef kebabs seasoned with tomato, coriander, and warming spices.

Ingredients

  • Minced beef — 500g
  • Tomato — 1 small, finely diced and drained
  • Onion — 1 small, finely chopped
  • Fresh coriander — ¼ cup, chopped
  • Green chili — 1, chopped
  • Crushed coriander seeds — 1 tsp
  • Cumin powder — ½ tsp
  • Red chili flakes — ½ tsp
  • Cornmeal or flour — 2 tbsp
  • Egg — 1
  • Salt and oil — as needed

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix minced beef with tomato, onion, coriander, green chili, spices, cornmeal, egg, and salt.
  2. Rest the mixture for 10 minutes so it firms up.
  3. Shape into thin, broad patties with lightly oiled hands.
  4. Heat a thin layer of oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  5. Fry patties for about 4–5 minutes per side until browned and cooked through to a safe internal temperature of 71°C (160°F).
  6. Drain briefly and serve hot with naan, chutney, and sliced onions.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting. Cooking times can vary with equipment and ingredient size; make sure meat and poultry are fully cooked before serving.
