Spicy Korean Gochujang Noodles

Glossy, spicy-sweet noodles tossed with garlic, vegetables, and Korean gochujang sauce.

Ingredients

  • Noodles — 200g
  • Gochujang Korean chili paste — 1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce — 1 tbsp
  • Honey or brown sugar — 1 tsp
  • Sesame oil — 1 tbsp
  • Garlic — 2 cloves, minced
  • Carrot — 1 small, thinly sliced
  • Spring onions — 2, sliced
  • Sesame seeds — 1 tsp
  • Neutral cooking oil — 1 tsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook noodles according to the packet instructions, then drain and reserve a little cooking water.
  2. Mix gochujang, soy sauce, honey, and sesame oil in a small bowl.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a pan and sauté garlic and carrot for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add the sauce and a splash of noodle water; stir until glossy.
  5. Toss in the cooked noodles until evenly coated and hot.
  6. Top with spring onions and sesame seeds, then serve immediately.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting. Cooking times can vary with equipment and ingredient size; make sure meat and poultry are fully cooked before serving.
