Chicken • 50 min • Medium

Moroccan Lemon Olive Chicken Tagine

Aromatic North African chicken braised with preserved lemons, green olives, saffron, ginger, and fresh coriander.

Ingredients

  • Chicken bone-in pieces (legs & thighs) — 800g
  • Preserved lemon — 1 (rind finely diced)
  • Pitted green Moroccan olives — ¾ cup rinsed
  • Yellow onion — 2 finely grated or chopped
  • Garlic cloves — 4 minced
  • Ground ginger & cumin — 1 tsp each
  • Turmeric & Saffron strands — ½ tsp each
  • Coriander & flat-leaf parsley — ½ cup chopped
  • Olive oil — 3 tbsp

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. In a tagine or heavy Dutch oven, heat olive oil over medium heat.
  2. Add grated onions, minced garlic, ginger, turmeric, cumin, and steeped saffron. Sauté for 3 minutes until aromatic.
  3. Add chicken pieces and sear on all sides for 8 minutes to absorb the fragrant spices.
  4. Pour in 1 cup of warm water and half the chopped herbs. Bring to a simmer.
  5. Cover tightly with lid, reduce heat to low, and braise gently for 35 minutes until chicken is meltingly tender.
  6. Add diced preserved lemon rind and green olives. Simmer uncovered for 8-10 minutes until the sauce reduces to a thick, glossy consistency.
  7. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve warm with crusty Moroccan bread or fluffy couscous.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
