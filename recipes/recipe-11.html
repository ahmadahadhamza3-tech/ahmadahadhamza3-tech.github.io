Gulab Jamun (Rose & Cardamom Syrup)

Soft, melt-in-the-mouth golden fried milk dumplings soaked in a warm, fragrant rosewater, saffron, and cardamom sugar syrup.

Ingredients

  • Full-cream milk powder — 1 cup
  • All-purpose flour (maida) — 2 tbsp
  • Baking powder — ¼ tsp
  • Desi Ghee — 1 tbsp + Ghee for deep frying
  • Warm whole milk — 4-5 tbsp
  • Sugar — 1½ cups
  • Water — 1½ cups
  • Cardamom pods — 4 cracked
  • Rose water — 1 tsp
  • Slivered pistachios & almonds — for garnish

Cooking time

35 min

Difficulty

Medium

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare syrup: Simmer sugar, water, cracked cardamom pods, and saffron strands for 6-8 minutes until sticky. Stir in rose water and keep warm.
  2. In a bowl, mix milk powder, flour, and baking powder. Rub in 1 tbsp ghee, then gradually add milk to form a soft, pliable dough.
  3. Cover and rest dough for 5 minutes, then shape into small, completely smooth crack-free balls.
  4. Heat ghee in a deep kadai over low-medium heat. Fry balls gently, continuously swirling the oil so they fry uniformly into a deep golden-brown color (8-10 minutes).
  5. Immediately drain and drop hot gulab jamuns into the warm sugar syrup.
  6. Let them soak for at least 45 minutes to 1 hour to absorb the sweet syrup completely. Garnish with slivered pistachios.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
