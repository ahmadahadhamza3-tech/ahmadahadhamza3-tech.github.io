Fresh Green Salad

Crisp vegetables with lemon for a fresh and healthy side.

Ingredients

  • Cucumber — 1
  • Tomatoes — 2
  • Lettuce
  • Onion — 1
  • Lemon juice — 1 tbsp
  • Salt — to taste

Cooking time

10 min

Difficulty

Easy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Wash all vegetables.
  2. Chop cucumber, tomatoes and onion.
  3. Add lettuce.
  4. Season with lemon juice and salt.
  5. Toss and serve fresh.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
