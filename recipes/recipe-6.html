Fluffy Pancakes

Soft, fluffy pancakes made for a perfect breakfast.

Ingredients

  • Flour — 1 cup
  • Milk — 1 cup
  • Egg — 1
  • Sugar — 2 tbsp
  • Baking powder — 1 tsp
  • Butter — 2 tbsp

Cooking time

20 min

Difficulty

Easy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix flour, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Add milk and egg; whisk until smooth.
  3. Heat a pan with butter.
  4. Pour batter and cook both sides until golden.
  5. Serve with honey or syrup.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
