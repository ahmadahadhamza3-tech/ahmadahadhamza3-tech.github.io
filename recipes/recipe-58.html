Crispy Golden Popcorn Chicken Bites

Bite-sized tender chicken breast pieces coated in seasoned buttermilk and crispy flour, fried to crunchy golden perfection.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken breast — 500g cut into bite-sized 1-inch cubes
  • Buttermilk — ¾ cup
  • All-purpose flour — 1 cup
  • Cornstarch — ¼ cup
  • Garlic powder, onion powder & paprika — 1 tsp each
  • White pepper & salt — 1 tsp each
  • Oil for deep frying
  • Honey mustard dip or BBQ sauce

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate bite-sized chicken cubes in seasoned buttermilk for 20 minutes.
  2. In a wide bowl, whisk flour, cornstarch, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and white pepper.
  3. Drizzle 2 tablespoons of buttermilk into the flour mix and rub with your fingers to form small crunchy flakes.
  4. Toss marinated chicken cubes in the seasoned flour mix, pressing firmly so each piece is coated with crunchy bits.
  5. Heat frying oil to 180°C (350°F).
  6. Fry popcorn chicken in small batches for 3-4 minutes until golden brown and extra crunchy. Drain on wire rack.
  7. Dust with a touch of sea salt and serve immediately with honey mustard or garlic dipping sauce.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
