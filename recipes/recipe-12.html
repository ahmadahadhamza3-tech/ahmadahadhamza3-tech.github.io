Avocado Toast with Poached Egg

Artisan sourdough toast topped with mashed seasoned avocado and a runny poached egg.

Ingredients

  • Sourdough bread — 2 slices
  • Ripe avocado — 1
  • Eggs — 2
  • Lemon juice — 1 tsp
  • Red chili flakes — ½ tsp
  • Sea salt & black pepper — to taste
  • Olive oil — 1 tsp

Cooking time

12 min

Difficulty

Easy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Toast sourdough slices until golden and crisp.
  2. Mash ripe avocado with lemon juice, salt, pepper, and olive oil.
  3. Poach eggs in simmering water for 3 minutes until whites are set and yolks are runny.
  4. Spread mashed avocado generously over warm toast.
  5. Top with poached eggs, chili flakes, and serve immediately.

Cooking Notes

Read all ingredients before starting, prepare your ingredients in advance, and adjust salt and spice gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat level, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
