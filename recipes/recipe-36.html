Dessert • 35 min • Easy

Warm Fudgy Chocolate Brownies

Dense, gooey chocolate brownies with glossy crinkly tops, loaded with melted dark chocolate chunks.

Ingredients

  • Dark chocolate (70%) — 200g
  • Unsalted butter — ½ cup (115g)
  • Granulated sugar — ¾ cup
  • Brown sugar — ½ cup
  • Eggs — 3 at room temperature
  • All-purpose flour — ½ cup
  • Dutch cocoa powder — ¼ cup
  • Vanilla extract — 1 tsp
  • Flaky sea salt — for sprinkling

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F) and line an 8-inch square pan with parchment paper.
  2. Melt dark chocolate and butter together in a heatproof bowl over simmering water until smooth. Cool slightly.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk eggs, granulated sugar, and brown sugar vigorously for 4-5 minutes until pale and frothy (this creates the shiny crinkle top!).
  4. Gently fold in the melted chocolate mixture and vanilla extract.
  5. Sift in flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Fold just until no dry streaks remain.
  6. Pour into prepared pan and bake for 22-25 minutes (center should still be slightly fudgy).
  7. Cool before slicing into squares. Serve with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Cooking Notes

Prepare ingredients before starting and adjust seasoning gradually to suit your taste. Cooking times can vary with equipment, ingredient size, and heat, so use the visual cues in the instructions as well as the stated time.
